Support

Questions, bugs, or ideas? Email support@glancehub.app and you will hear back within two days.

Tapping unlock on a locked app does nothing

Apple does not let the lock screen open another app. Tapping the button sends a Phos notification. Tap that notification to go straight to your reading. If no notification appears, turn on notifications for Phos in Settings, Notifications, Phos.

My apps are not locking

Open Phos, then Settings, and make sure Screen Time access is allowed and at least one app is chosen under Locked apps. Screen Time must also be turned on in the iPhone Settings app.

I want to change the rules

Open Settings, then Unlock rules. Stricter changes apply right away. Easier changes wait 24 hours, so a tired moment cannot undo your plan. You can turn that wait off, and turning it off also waits 24 hours.

I really need my apps right now

Use an emergency pass from the Today screen or Settings. Each pass waits 60 seconds, then opens your apps for up to 30 minutes. Phone, Messages, and Maps are never locked.

The questions do not match my Bible

Questions follow the World English Bible. Most translations say the same thing in slightly different words, so pick the answer that means what your Bible says.

Removing Phos

Deleting the app removes every lock and all of your data. You can also remove Screen Time access in Settings, Screen Time, Apps with Screen Time Access.
