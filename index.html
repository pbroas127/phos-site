Keep your light burning

Your distracting apps stay locked each morning until you read a chapter of the Bible, reflect on it, and answer a few questions about it.

  1. Apps lock at the time you choose.
  2. Read today's chapter in your own Bible, in the app, or out loud.
  3. Reflect by typing or speaking. Paste is turned off.
  4. Answer questions written for that chapter.
  5. Apps open for as long as you set.
