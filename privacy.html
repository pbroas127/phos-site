Wick

Privacy

Wick collects nothing. Your data stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud.

Last updated September 14, 2026

No accounts, no servers, no tracking

Wick has no sign in, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers. The developer never receives your data.

Screen Time

Wick uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks to lock the apps you choose. Apple gives Wick private tokens instead of app names, so Wick cannot see which apps you picked or how you use them. The weekly usage chart is drawn by Apple on your device and is never available to Wick.

Reflections and speech

Reflections you type or speak are saved only on your iPhone in your journal. Spoken reflections are converted to text using Apple speech recognition, on your device whenever your iPhone supports it. When on device recognition is not available, Apple may process the audio under Apple's own privacy policy. Wick never uploads or shares your recordings or text.

iCloud backup

If you are signed in to iCloud, Wick saves a copy of your readings, journal, streaks, trophies, and settings to your own iCloud account using Apple's iCloud key value storage, so you can restore them on a new iPhone. The developer cannot access your iCloud. You can turn off iCloud for Wick in the Settings app.

Natural voices

If you choose a natural voice, Wick downloads the open source Kokoro voice model from Hugging Face. The download sends no personal information, and the voices then run entirely on your iPhone.

Notifications

Wick sends local notifications from your iPhone, such as the one that opens your reading from a locked app and an optional daily reading reminder you can turn off in Settings. No push service is used.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to erase everything it stored on your iPhone. To remove the iCloud backup, turn off iCloud for Wick in the Settings app or manage iCloud storage.

Children

Wick does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
