Phos

Privacy

Phos collects nothing. Everything stays on your iPhone.

Last updated September 12, 2026

No accounts, no servers, no tracking

Phos has no sign in, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers. The developer never receives your data.

Screen Time

Phos uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks to lock the apps you choose. Apple gives Phos private tokens instead of app names, so Phos cannot see which apps you picked or how you use them. The weekly usage chart is drawn by Apple on your device and is never available to Phos.

Reflections and speech

Reflections you type or speak are saved only on your iPhone in your journal. Spoken reflections are converted to text using Apple speech recognition, on your device whenever your iPhone supports it. When on device recognition is not available, Apple may process the audio under Apple's own privacy policy. Phos never uploads or shares your recordings or text.

Motion

Notifications

Phos sends local notifications from your iPhone, such as the one that opens your reading from a locked app. No push service is used.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to erase everything it stored.

Children

Phos does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
