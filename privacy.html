Wick

Privacy

Wick collects nothing. Your data stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud.

Last updated September 16, 2026

No accounts, no servers, no tracking

Wick has no sign in, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers. The developer never receives your data.

Screen Time

Wick uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks to lock the apps you choose. Apple gives Wick private tokens instead of app names, so Wick cannot see which apps you picked or how you use them. The weekly usage chart is drawn by Apple on your device and is never available to Wick.

Reflections and speech

Reflections you type or speak are saved only on your iPhone in your journal, along with the recording of a spoken reflection so you can play it back. Spoken reflections are converted to text using Apple speech recognition, on your device whenever your iPhone supports it. When on device recognition is not available, Apple may process the audio under Apple's own privacy policy. Wick never uploads your recordings or text. Recordings are not part of the iCloud backup.

iCloud backup

If you are signed in to iCloud, Wick saves a copy of your readings, journal, reviews, streaks, trophies, and settings to your own iCloud account using Apple's iCloud key value storage, so you can restore them on a new iPhone. The developer cannot access your iCloud. You can turn backup off in Wick's Settings. Your app choices and lock protection are never included, because Screen Time keeps them on your iPhone.

Natural voices

If you choose a natural voice, Wick downloads the open source Kokoro voice model from Hugging Face. The download sends no personal information, and the voices then run entirely on your iPhone.

Bible translations

The World English Bible comes with the app. If you choose another translation, Wick downloads that public domain text from the developer's GitHub releases. The download sends no personal information.

Sharing and export

Your weekly summary card and journal export are made on your iPhone. They leave it only if you send them yourself with the share sheet.

Notifications

Wick sends local notifications from your iPhone: the one that opens your reading from a locked app, an optional daily reading reminder, and a heads up before a lock change or passcode reset takes effect. No push service is used.

Deleting your data

In Wick, Settings, Your data, Delete all my data erases everything Wick stored on your iPhone and, if you choose, your iCloud backup. Deleting the app also erases what it stored on your iPhone. You can export your journal first from the same screen.

Children

Wick does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
